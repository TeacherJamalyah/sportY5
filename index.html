⚡ Grammar 1: Using 'PLAY', 'DO', and 'GO'

In English, sports verbs are categorized into 3 main groups:

⚽

PLAY

For team sports or competitive games with a **BALL / EQUIPMENT**:

  • • Play football ⚽
  • • Play basketball 🏀
  • • Play tennis 🎾
  • • Play badminton 🏸
🥋

DO

For **INDIVIDUAL EXERCISES** or **MARTIAL ARTS** (no ball):

  • • Do gymnastics 🤸
  • • Do taekwondo 🥋
  • • Do athletics 🏃
  • • Do karate 🥋
🏊

GO

For sports activities that end with **'-ING'**:

  • • Go swimming 🏊
  • • Go cycling 🚴
  • • Go skiing 🎿
  • • Go snowboarding 🏂

🎯 Grammar 2: Expressing Ability & Skill

Essential grammar rules commonly tested in UASA examinations:

1. Good at / Bad at + Verb (-ing)

Always use Verb + **-ing** after the phrase *good at* or *bad at*.

✅ I am good at swimming.

❌ I am good at swim. (Incorrect)

✅ He is bad at running.

2. Can / Can't + Base Verb

Use the Base Verb (original form without '-ing' or '-s') after *can* or *can't*.

✅ She can kick the ball high.

❌ She can kicking the ball. (Incorrect)

✅ They can't swim fast.
